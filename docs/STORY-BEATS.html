STORY

PROBLEM → SESSION → MIX → TRUST.

  1. 01 / PRESSURE

    THE TEMPLATE IS THE WEATHER.

    Most records arrive pre-mixed: playlist-safe drums, stock silk, no negative space. The artist walks into a room that already decided the feeling. 36TY exists because that weather is the problem — a Brooklyn hip-hop / R&B practice for people who still want a print left in the pocket.

  2. 02 / SESSION

    THE DAY IS THE SESSION.

    Live in the room or remote on the line. Four hours. Drums first, silk second, hook last. MPC 2000XL, Rhodes, SP-404, NS-10s. The pocket is negotiated, not downloaded — pressure, air for the vocal, and a temperature change you can actually hear.

  3. 03 / MIX

    THE MIX IS A LANDSCAPE.

    Low-end that sits like weather. Midrange in half-light. Tops like condensation on glass. Boom-bap posture, R&B melt — no nostalgia costume. The mercury object in the hero is that landscape made visible. On this page the tapes are real Web Audio sketches, not a poster of a player.

  4. 04 / TRUST

    THE LOCK IS THE RELATIONSHIP.

    Custom beats. Live and remote sessions. Non-exclusive leases and exclusive buyouts. Sync cues against picture lock. Booking stays a real HTML form — mailto to booking@36ty.world — not a canvas you cannot submit through. Tempo, reference, emotional weather. That is enough to start.
