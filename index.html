02 / INFLUENCE

THE STREET ALREADY HAD A BASSLINE.

Heat on zinc. Hills holding the back of the mix. Sound-system pressure — not a postcard, not a resort. Origin as climate: the ear learned outdoors first.

03 / MOVEMENT

WHAT YOU KEEP ON THE ROAD.

Vegetation thins. The road regularizes. Warm haze cools. This is the crossing — Jamaica receding behind the camera, another grid assembling in front of it.

04 / TORONTO

BRICK. NIGHT BUS. WINTER AIR.

A colder city that taught the record to sit. Bay windows. Concrete. A needle on the skyline. Not a screensaver — a street you drop into, then lift out of.

05 / CREATION

MPC 2000XL. Rhodes. SP-404. Four-hour sessions: drums first, silk second, hook last. 36TY is the room that holds both climates — outdoor pressure and winter grid — in one pocket.

Leases, exclusives, sessions, sync. Tempo, reference, weather. The road ends in a quiet destination — still HTML, still sendable if the world goes dark.
