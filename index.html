01 / MANIFESTO

INK FIRST. COLOR SECOND. POCKET ALWAYS.

36TY is a Brooklyn producer building records the way 1994 built comics: thick ink, loud color, no apology. The drums are boom-bap — dusty, swung, slightly behind the beat on purpose. The harmony is R&B — minor-7 silk, gospel inversions, chords that sit in the chest.

The alias started as a late-night crate ritual. MPC 2000XL. A milk crate of '92–'99 vinyl. A cheap Rhodes plugin that got replaced by the real thing. Now the booth is a full room and the sound is still the same argument: the snare has to knock, the hook has to melt.

Not nostalgia. Archaeology with a new mix bus. 90s hip-hop grammar, 90s comic framing, 2026 low-end.
